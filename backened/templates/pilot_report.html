2. KPI Definitions & Method

  • Availability — modelled from incident load (each open critical incident costs 0.25%, high risk 0.10%).
  • SLA compliance — measured availability vs the 99.5% target.
  • Alert reduction — (raw alerts - correlated events) / raw alerts; repeated alarms inside a 5-minute window are coalesced.
  • MTTR — (resolve timestamp - detect timestamp) averaged over resolved incidents; resolve incidents to populate this metric.
